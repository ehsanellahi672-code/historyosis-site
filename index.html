One app for the ward round and the clinic with no internet

The same structured intake serves two very different needs. Choose the one that sounds like you.

Students and trainees

An active-recall companion for wards and OSCEs

Instead of rereading a checklist, you recall and record. The app lays out every question and examination step in the order a clinician thinks, so a thorough history becomes a habit and nothing gets missed.

  • Tap structured finding chips to log signs in real time as you examine.
  • Student mode adds teaching hints and a "Before you finish" checklist.
  • Red-flag lists and Must-not-miss causes train you to ask the question that matters.
  • Supervisors can comment on any section, then approve and lock the record.

Low-resource clinics and hospitals

Offline-ready clinical records on the devices you already have

There is no server to set up, no licence to buy to try it, and no connection to lose. It runs on phones, tablets and budget PCs, and saves records in the browser's local storage on that device.

  • Works after the first load with no internet, including all medical content.
  • Records stay on the device. Nothing is sent to us, and there are no accounts.
  • Clinician mode is lean: fewer prompts, with safety alerts always shown.
  • Print a black-and-white A4 record, a discharge summary, or copy as text.

Be aware: records live on one device and are not encrypted by the app. Use device encryption and a screen lock. Sharing between devices is not built yet.

What's new

Thirty-seven updates so far, grouped into the four things you will notice. The latest is v3.37.1. All screens and examples use fictional patients.

Examination

Enhanced physical exam coverage

  • 23 examination systems as grouped tap chips: cardiovascular, respiratory, abdomen, neurological, psychiatric, eye, ENT, thyroid, breast, skin, groin, obstetric, paediatric and more.
  • Musculoskeletal screens for GALS, hip, knee, ankle and foot, shoulder, hand and wrist, and cervical and lumbar spine.
  • Red-flag signs raise an alert, and "Mark all normal" never touches sensitive or special tests.
  • One-tap "Suggested for your complaint" exam buttons.
  • Numeric lab values with units and Normal, High, Low and Critical flags.
Reasoning

Dynamic DDx logic

  • A rule-based differential for all 76 complaints, from chest pain to fever in a child.
  • Must-not-miss causes kept visible in Student and Clinician mode.
  • Live updates as you tap, in under 15 ms on average for several complaints at once.
  • Student mode shows the reasoning. Clinician mode shows a compact list.
Documentation

Streamlined export and discharge

  • A discharge summary pre-filled from the record, with medications marked New, Changed, Continued or Stopped.
  • Allergy warnings on discharge medicines, and explicit "None recorded" where a field is empty.
  • Redesigned black-and-white A4 print that photocopies cleanly.
  • Print or save as PDF, copy text, or download a .txt file.
Experience

Performance and accessibility

  • A new home screen with Resume draft and recent records, plus bottom navigation.
  • Student and Clinician modes, eight colour presets, a custom colour, dark mode and High contrast.
  • Forced-colours support, 400% zoom without sideways scrolling, 44 px touch targets, labelled regions for screen readers.
  • One-tap Undo after removals, and an install prompt for offline use.

Open beta: help us make it safer and sharper

HistoryOsis is in open beta and gets better with every real-world edge case. We are inviting three groups to work with us.

  • Clinicians to test examination and differential content against real practice, and tell us what is wrong or missing.
  • Medical students to use it on wards and in OSCE practice, and tell us where it slows you down.
  • Front-end developers to report bugs, review accessibility and collaborate on upcoming clinical workflows.

Use fictional patients while testing. Clinical content is draft until reviewed.
