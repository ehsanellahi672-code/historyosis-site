What's new in the app

Release notes in plain words, newest first. The version on this site is v3.37.1.

  1. v3.37.1

    Accessibility fix in the differentials list

    The Add button no longer sits inside a clickable row, so screen readers and switch access announce and activate it correctly. Looks and works the same.

  2. v3.37

    Final accessibility pass

    Forced-colours and high-contrast support, 400% zoom without sideways scrolling, 44 px touch targets, labelled controls and an accessibility statement in Settings.

  3. v3.36

    Appearance settings

    Eight colour presets, a custom colour and High contrast. Printed records always use a plain light page.

  4. v3.35

    Saved Records tiles and Undo

    Summary tiles, helpful empty states, one-tap delete with Undo, and a collapse bar for one-handed use on the ward.

  5. v3.34

    Home screen and bottom navigation

    A Home screen with Start, Resume draft and recent records, bottom navigation between Home, Intake and Records, and an extended New Intake button.

  6. v3.32 to v3.33

    New visual design

    A Material 3 teal design system across the app, with tonal vitals and a contrast scan of every screen.

  7. v3.30 to v3.31

    Discharge summary

    Pre-filled from the record and editable. Medicines are marked New, Changed, Continued or Stopped, with allergy warnings. Print, save as PDF, copy text or download a .txt file.

  8. v3.21 to v3.29

    Differential diagnosis

    The engine, the panel in Section E, and rule sets that grew until all 76 presenting complaints were covered. Version 3.29.1 made it name only the findings a patient actually has.

  9. v3.20

    Lab values and range flags

    Numeric analytes with units and Normal, High, Low and Critical flags.

  10. v3.12 to v3.19

    Examination chips

    Tap chips for 23 body systems, with red-flag alerts, "Mark all normal" and suggested examinations for each complaint.
