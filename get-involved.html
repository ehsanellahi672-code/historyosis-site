Get involved

HistoryOsis is in open beta. Clinicians, students and developers can help now, and one class, one clinic or one ward is enough for a pilot.

Open beta: help us make it safer and sharper

HistoryOsis is in open beta and gets better with every real-world edge case. We are inviting three groups to work with us.

  • Clinicians to test examination and differential content against real practice, and tell us what is wrong or missing.
  • Medical students to use it on wards and in OSCE practice, and tell us where it slows you down.
  • Front-end developers to report bugs, review accessibility and collaborate on upcoming clinical workflows.

Use fictional patients while testing. Clinical content is draft until reviewed.

Start with a small pilot

One class, one skills lab, one clinic or one ward is enough to learn what works.

  1. Try the app

    Open it on your phone. There is no account to create.

  2. Tell us what you teach or treat

    We can add your complaints, questions, checklists and templates, and your faculty can review the examination steps with us.

  3. Run it with one group

    You tell us what slows people down. That decides what we build next.

Bring HistoryOsis to your institution

Write to us with your institution, who would use it, and what you want to improve. We reply personally.
