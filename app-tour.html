Home/App tour

A tour of the app, one screen at a time

These are real screens from v3.37.1, captured with a fictional patient. The order follows a clinical encounter: start, take the history, examine, reason, check, sign off and discharge.

Light or dark? The screens follow this site's theme. Use the Theme button at the top of any page to switch.

Start

Open the app on a phone and you land on Home. Bottom navigation takes you between Home, the Intake form and your saved Records.

Take the history

Each presenting complaint opens its own questions as taps. For symptoms, one tap is present and two is absent.

Examine

Vital signs get plain-word tags as you type them. Examination systems are grouped chips, with red-flag alerts.

Reason it through

The differential panel reads what you have entered and keeps the dangerous causes in view. You choose what to add.

Check before you prescribe

Warnings are advisory. They ask you to confirm that you checked, and they never stop a record from saving.

Review and sign off

A supervisor or consultant can comment, approve and lock a record. Every step is logged.

Discharge

A discharge summary is filled in from the record. You edit it, then print, save as PDF, copy the text or download a .txt file.

Make it yours

Choose a colour preset or your own colour, switch to dark, raise contrast or text size. Printing always uses a plain light page.

Try it on your own phone

There is no account and nothing to install. Open the app, start a patient, and add it to your home screen if you want it offline.
